help_center_article: |
  ## Why edit a customer's details?

  When you first add a customer, you usually only enter their name and phone
  number to get started quickly. As you work with them, you'll often want to
  fill in more — an email address, their shop's location, or terms like how
  many days they usually take to pay.

  ## Steps

  Open the customer from the Customers list — click their name to go to their
  page. On the Overview tab (the page you land on by default), scroll past
  the balance and order stats to find their profile form, split into
  Contact, Business, and Terms sections. Update any fields you like — Email,
  Address, Company name, Payment terms (days), Credit limit, and more are all
  here. Click "Save profile" to keep your changes.

  ## Tip

  None of these fields are required — BantooBooks only asks for a name (and
  optionally a phone number) when you first add a customer. Everything else,
  including WhatsApp, Country, and City set at creation time, can always be
  changed later from this same form.
